# Project Descriptions & Updates
 
 **PICOMF**<br>
##### Research Overview
Magnetic fields in the primordial Universe may have left observable imprints. These primordial magnetic fields (PMFs) may be used as a probe for early-Universe Physics. Our current probes (e.g. gravitational waves and neutrinos) are either incompletely observed, or difficult to measure. PMFs may be observed in the following ways:
- their evolution maybe leave imprints on the CMB spectral distortion (data for this wasn't measured by Planck – the earliest observations we have come from COBE)
- magnetic fields may remain relatively unaltered within cosmic voids, and we will get better constraints on PMFs from void observations in the coming years (CTA). Within voids, Blazar photons with TeV energies decay into electron-positron pairs after a mean free path of ~100 Mpc, and interact with the CMB via inverse compton scattering. In the presence of PMFs, these pairs will be deflected and therefore not observed along the line-of-sight, but instead in a halo around the blazar. <br>
The PENCIL code performs MHD simulations including inflationary PMBs as initial conditions. PMBs and their evolution is sensitive to the chosen inflationary model, meaning that their observational detection will allow us to better constrain inflation.
